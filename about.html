About This Project

Bridging the gap between mathematical theory and intuition.

Engineering concepts are often mathematically intensive and difficult to interpret. This project focuses on transforming abstract ideas into interactive visualizations, making learning more intuitive and engaging.

Instead of only solving equations on paper, users can explore how systems behave in real time—from control systems and signal processing to advanced digital architecture.

The Architect

Anish Rooj

I am an Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering student at Jadavpur University with a strong interest in digital design, computer architecture, and system-level engineering.

My work focuses on building systems from the ground up to understand how theory translates into hardware. I have designed and implemented 32-bit RISC-V processors, working from high-level HDL descriptions down to gate-level implementations using fundamental logic components.

Through these projects, I have developed a deep understanding of datapath design, control logic, instruction execution, and hardware verification. This platform was created to share that practical understanding with other students and engineers.

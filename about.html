About This Project

Engineering concepts are often mathematically intensive and difficult to interpret. This project focuses on transforming those abstract ideas into interactive visualizations, making learning more intuitive and engaging.

Instead of only solving equations, users can explore how systems behave in real time— from control systems and signal processing to digital architecture.

What This Platform Offers

  • Interactive visualizations for core engineering topics
  • Real-time system behavior exploration
  • Bridging theory with intuition
  • Tools for students to experiment and learn actively

About the Developer

I am Anish Rooj, an Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering student at Jadavpur University with a strong interest in digital design, computer architecture, and system-level engineering.

My work focuses on building systems from the ground up to understand how theory translates into hardware. I have designed and implemented 32-bit RISC-V processors, working from high-level HDL descriptions down to gate-level implementations using fundamental logic components.

Through these projects, I have developed a deep understanding of datapath design, control logic, instruction execution, and hardware verification.

This platform was created to bridge the gap between mathematical theory and practical understanding, by turning complex engineering concepts into interactive visual experiences.

You can explore more of my work on GitHub.

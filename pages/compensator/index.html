Key Concepts
Lead Compensator Adds phase lead near crossover. Used to increase phase margin and speed up response.
Lag Compensator Boosts low-frequency gain. Improves steady-state accuracy without destabilizing.
Phase Margin Target Typically aim for 45–60° phase margin for good transient response.

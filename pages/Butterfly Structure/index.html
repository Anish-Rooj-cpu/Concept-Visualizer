How it Works

The Cooley–Tukey algorithm recursively breaks down a Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) of size N into two smaller DFTs of size N/2.

Key Concepts:

  • Bit-Reversal: Notice the inputs on the left x[n]. They are not in order (0, 1, 2...). They are bit-reversed to allow in-place computation.
  • Stages: There are exactly log2(N) stages of computation.
  • The Butterfly (✖): Two inputs combine to create two outputs.

    Top Output: A + W · B
    Bottom Output: A − W · B
  • Twiddle Factor: The complex exponential multiplier WNk applied to the bottom branch.

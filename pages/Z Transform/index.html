DSP Intuition Guide:
• Inside Unit Circle: Signal decays (Stable).
• Outside Unit Circle: Signal grows infinitely (Unstable).
• Angle (θ): Determines the digital frequency of oscillation.
• Zeros on Unit Circle: Completely blocks that specific frequency.

Key Concepts
Definition X(z) = Σ x[n]·z⁻ⁿ
Stability For causal systems: all poles must lie strictly inside the unit circle |z| < 1.
Unit Circle = DTFT Evaluating X(z) on |z|=1 gives the Discrete-Time Fourier Transform.

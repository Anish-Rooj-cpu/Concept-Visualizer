What am I seeing?

The Big Idea: Any repeating wave can be broken down into a sum of basic sine waves.

Watch the circles (epicycles). The first circle is the fundamental frequency. Each attached circle spins faster, representing higher harmonics. Their vertical positions add up to draw the wave!

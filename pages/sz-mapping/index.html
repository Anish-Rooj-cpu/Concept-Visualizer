Key Concepts
Bilinear Transform s = (2/T)·(z−1)/(z+1). Maps entire jω axis to unit circle, no aliasing.
Stability Preserved Left-half s-plane maps inside the unit circle — stability is preserved.
Frequency Warping Bilinear introduces frequency warping; pre-warp critical frequencies using Ω = (2/T)·tan(ωT/2).

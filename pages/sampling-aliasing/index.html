Key Concepts
Nyquist Rate Sample at least 2× the highest frequency component to avoid aliasing.
Aliasing Effect Frequencies above fs/2 fold back and appear as lower-frequency imposters.
Anti-Aliasing A low-pass filter before the ADC removes frequencies above the Nyquist limit.
